## How to read the decisions

- **raw**: the reviewer's own verdict (`request_changes` = block).
- **pipeline**: what System Factory decides after executing every check and probe and classifying the evidence (ARCHITECTURE.md, evidence states). `escalated` means the evidence was unsupported, conflicting or the review did not inspect the change; the workflow goes to a human instead of approving or blocking.
- A *false approval* ships a labelled defect; a *false block* rejects a correct change.
